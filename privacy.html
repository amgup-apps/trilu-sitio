Trilu Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Leer en español

Trilu ("the app") is a gratitude journal: each day you write down three good things. This policy explains what happens with your information.

What the app stores

Your journal entries (including which 7-day challenge, if any, a day counted toward), the photos you choose to add, and your settings (language, reminders, colors, lock, and the last time you were reminded to back up). All of it is stored only on your phone, inside the app. The app also keeps two small technical things on your phone: if it ever closes because of an error, a short note with the app version, the Android version, the phone model and the error, which is shown to you and shared only if you tap Share; and counters so it does not ask you twice to rate or recommend it. Neither includes anything from your journal.

What we collect

Nothing. Trilu has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your journal, your photos or anything about you. Everything except purchases works without an internet connection.

Photos

You add photos with your phone's camera or photo picker; the app does not ask for camera or gallery permissions. Trilu keeps a smaller copy inside the app and removes location and other hidden data from it. Your original photos are not changed.

Purchases and subscriptions

Trilu includes a 60-day free trial that needs no card. After that, you can subscribe (monthly or yearly) or buy lifetime access through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play. Without a purchase you can still read, search, export and back up your journal.

Sharing, exports and backups

Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

  • Backup file and exports (backup .zip, text or PDF): created when you tap them and saved where you choose.
  • Automatic backup (optional, from version 1.0.18): if you choose a folder in Settings › Backup (for example on Drive, in Downloads or on an SD card), Trilu saves a copy of your journal and photos there as trilu-copia.zip, every 7 days when you open the app. It is saved only in the folder you chose, with the permission Android gives for that folder; you can change the folder or turn this off at any time. The file has no lock: keep that folder private.
  • Share one good thing (a picture card): only when you tap Share. Trilu makes a picture of the one good thing you choose, with a small Trilu mark, keeps it for a short time in the app's temporary storage (it is deleted automatically within a day) and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it. Trilu never sends it anywhere itself, and nothing else from your journal is included.
  • Recommend Trilu (recommend to a friend): only opens your phone's share menu with a short message and a link to the app. Nothing is sent to anyone unless you choose an app and send the message. Your journal is never included.
  • Android backup: if it is turned on, Android may include your journal text in your personal Google backup, under Google's terms. Photos and the lock are not included in that backup.
  • Rate on Google Play: Google Play may show its own review window. Google Play shows and handles it, and Trilu receives no personal data from that window.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the daily reminder you choose.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint: only if you turn on the lock with your fingerprint. Your fingerprint never leaves Android's secure system.
  • Internet and network status, Google Play billing: used only by Google Play to show prices, process purchases and show the review window.

The lock

Your PIN is stored on your phone in a protected, irreversible form, never in backups or exports. While the lock is on, the app's content is hidden in the recent-apps screen and in screenshots.

Deleting your data

You can delete any day in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything stored on the phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you put them.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom

The person responsible for this app (the "controller") is its independent developer, who you can reach at pedalup180@gmail.com. Trilu does not collect or transfer personal data: your journal, photos and settings stay on your phone, so there is nothing we could access, correct, delete or hand over for you. You exercise those rights directly in the app: you can read, export, change and delete your data at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Purchases are processed by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy, on the legal basis of performing the contract you choose to enter. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can write to us or complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Accessibility

Trilu follows the text size and dark mode of your phone, works with TalkBack and respects the "remove animations" setting. If something does not work for you, write to us and we will try to fix it.

Children

Trilu is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
